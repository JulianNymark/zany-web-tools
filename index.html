Small tools, one job each

Static pages that do one thing and can be opened, forked or linked to. No build step, no framework, no accounts — just HTML, CSS and a little JavaScript, styled with Designsystemet.

Tools

live map

Park & tree coverage sampler

Drop a pin and see how much of the square around it is parks and woodland — the “30” of the 3-30-300 rule. Sample it as a zoom square (neighbourhood, 5-minute walk) or a fixed buffer.

live webcam

Webcam eye tracking

On-device gaze estimation from a plain webcam. Calibrate to nine points, then pop a deterministic grid of bubbles with your eyes — the same layout and sizes every run. Also compares the browser eye-tracking libraries that exist.
